## Lettering + lino account

Something to watch while the ink dries.

Glass marbles, two staircases, a slide, a lift back to the top. Every landing plays a note. It never ends, which is either soothing or a little sinister depending on the day.

Took me an embarrassing while to learn that every step has to sit a hair lower than the last one. Line them up perfectly and the marbles just stop, stuck on an edge you can't even see.

## UX with intent

I built a marble run with real physics so I'd stop doomscrolling other people's.

The one thing I learned: every piece has to start a little lower than the one before it. When I lined them up exactly, marbles stalled on lips too small to see. I ended up adding a safety net that quietly sends any stuck marble back to the lift, which felt like cheating until I realised that's most of what good error handling is.
