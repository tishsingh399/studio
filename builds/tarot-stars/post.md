## @tish_letters (hand-drawn)

Pin art, but the pins are tiny tarot stars.

Dark parts of the picture push forward as sparkles, the middle tones become five-point stars, and the light parts sink back as little crescent moons. It's close to how I think about a halftone when I'm cutting, just with the depth turned up.

You can feed it your own photo. Nothing gets uploaded.

## @ux_perince (UX)

I made a thing that rebuilds any picture out of 3D stars.

The one thing I learned: my first version tilted the whole wall at an angle because it looked more impressively 3D. You couldn't see the picture anymore. Turning it to face you head on, and letting a raking light do the depth, is what made it work. I keep relearning that the clever angle is usually for me, not for whoever's looking.

Code is in my studio repo, link in bio.
