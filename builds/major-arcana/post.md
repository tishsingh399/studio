## @tish_letters (hand-drawn)

The 1909 Rider-Waite-Smith deck, all 22 trump cards, in black and white.

Pamela Colman Smith drew these more than a hundred years ago and they're public domain now. I built a little page to draw a card a day, and a switch that re-renders her linework as engraving, halftone or star dither. The engraving one looks the most like something I'd want to carve.

## @ux_perince (UX)

My first tarot cards were drawn in code. They looked like a demo.

The one thing I learned: code-drawn art has a ceiling, and I hit it fast. The fix wasn't better code. It was using real illustration, the 1909 Rider-Waite-Smith deck, which had been in the public domain the whole time. Now the code does what it's good at: drawing a card, laying out a reading, restyling the art.

Still thinking about how often I reach for building something when the thing already exists.

Code is in my studio repo, link in bio.
