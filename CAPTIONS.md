# Captions

Captions should sound like me talking to one friend about something I made. If a line could sit under anyone's post, cut it.

## Shape

1. What it is, plus one real detail from making it. On a reel, that line goes on screen too.
2. One or two lines only I could write: what fought me, what surprised me, what I'd change. Claude leaves these as [brackets]; I fill them.
3. Materials, if it's a print: block, ink, paper.
4. Five tags at most: #linocut #linoprint #printmaking #reliefprint #handcarved

## Never

- Em dashes, emojis, hype words: stunning, vibrant, journey, passion, elevate, delve, showcase.
- Lessons or advice at the end ("remember to...", "the takeaway is...").
- Feelings or experiences I didn't say I had. If Claude doesn't know, it writes a bracket.
- "Link in bio" more than once, and never as the whole ending.

## Example

> Kamal, carved backwards.
> [what the letters were like to carve]
> [block], purple ink, [paper].
> #linocut #linoprint #printmaking
